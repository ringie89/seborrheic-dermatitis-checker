Rating criteria

The overall rating reflects the highest caution level found. It is not a percentage or an estimate of medical risk.

0/3

No flagged ingredients

No active screening rule matched. Based on this check, the product appears suitable for a Malassezia-conscious routine; individual tolerance cannot be guaranteed.

3/3

High caution

Includes selected long-chain free fatty acids, botanical oils, and Galactomyces under conservative screening rules.

2/3

Moderate caution

Includes certain long-chain fatty acid esters, lipid-based ingredients, and the exact Cetearyl Alcohol label because one in-vitro study reported a strain-specific growth signal. This does not establish a clinical trigger.

1/3

Low-confidence flag

Unverified fatty-acid-related compounds, selected ferments, a preliminary Squalene mechanism, and the exact Mineral Oil / Paraffinum Liquidum labels based on conflicting in-vitro results (strict mode only).

Mode behavior: Strict screening includes low-confidence 1/3 flags, Galactomyces (3/3), and unknown oil/wax name-pattern flags. Balanced screening hides those rule types.

Interpretation: This checker flags only Malassezia- or seborrheic-dermatitis-related concerns. Perfume, alcohol, preservatives, and surfactants are not scored just for general irritation or allergy. Laboratory findings do not establish clinical flare-up risk.

No flagged ingredients means no active screening rule matched. It does not guarantee that the product will be suitable for everyone.

Limitations

The checker cannot determine ingredient concentrations, the complete chemistry of a formulation, or your personal triggers. It cannot recognize every possible ingredient name.

Ratings are deliberately conservative, not a diagnosis or a medical recommendation. Seek dermatological advice for persistent or severe symptoms.
