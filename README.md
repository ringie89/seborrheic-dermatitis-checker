# Seborrheic Dermatitis Product Checker

[Open the product checker](https://ringie89.github.io/seborrheic-dermatitis-checker/)

A free, browser-based tool for screening shampoo, cleanser, and skincare ingredient lists for possible seborrheic-dermatitis relevance. Paste an ingredient list to see potential matches, caution levels, and brief explanations.

Ingredient analysis runs locally in your browser. Results are informational only: they cannot predict an individual's skin response, confirm a trigger, or replace medical advice.
